NFL Similator

NFL Sim is an interactive NFL-style season simulator built with Python and Flask. Users can create a full 18-week season, simulate individual games or entire seasons, follow team standings and statistics, build playoff brackets, and crown a Super Bowl champion. The project includes customizable team ratings, weather effects, realistic football scoring, and a browser-based control room for managing the simulation.
